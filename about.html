Our Heritage

From a Backyard Playgroup to an Accredited Nature Sanctuary

What began in 2006 as a small gathering of curious neighborhood toddlers exploring ladybugs in Ms. Maria's sensory garden has blossomed into one of Texas's most celebrated Reggio-inspired early learning academies.

While our campus has grown to feature light-filled ateliers, an organic teaching orchard, and state-of-the-art security, our core heartbeat remains unchanged: every child is born whole, curious, and deserving of unconditional respect.
