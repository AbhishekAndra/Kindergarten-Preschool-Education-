# Little Sprout Academy - Kindergarten & Preschool Website

A fully responsive, multi-page preschool and kindergarten website built with **React 19**, **Vite**, **Tailwind CSS v4**, and **Lucide React**.

## 🌟 Pages & Routes Implemented

1. **Home (`/`)**
   - Announcement Top Banner with 2026 Admissions notice
   - Navigation with active page indicators & mobile drawer menu
   - Hero Section with headline, floating badge, dual CTAs, stats strip & trust seals
   - 4 Value Pillars ("Why Families Choose Little Sprout")
   - Program Overview Cards with age-group filter pills
   - Interactive Learning Spaces & Campus Showcase with photo tabs
   - Parent Testimonials with 5-star ratings & verified badges
   - Big Call-to-Action Banner & Rich Comprehensive Footer

2. **About (`/about`)**
   - School Mission, Vision, and Reggio Emilia + Montessori philosophy
   - 14-Year Interactive Journey Timeline (2012 to 2026)
   - Certified Faculty & Leadership Showcase with credentials & favorite classroom activities
   - Campus Health & Safety Standards (HEPA-13 air purifiers, biometrics, 100% CPR certified)

3. **Programs (`/programs`)**
   - 6 Detailed Age Programs:
     - Infant Nest (6 Weeks – 14 Months)
     - Toddler Explorers (14 Months – 2.5 Years)
     - Early Preschool Sparks (2.5 – 3.5 Years)
     - Pre-Kindergarten STEAM (3.5 – 5 Years)
     - Junior Kindergarten & Prep (5 – 6 Years)
     - Enrichment & Summer Camp (3 – 8 Years)
   - Daily Rhythm Timelines, Milestones, and Ratio badges
   - Side-by-Side Program Comparison Matrix
   - Included Specialty Academies (Spanish Immersion, Orff Music, STEM Bee-Bots, Organic Gardening, Yoga)

4. **Curriculum (`/curriculum`)**
   - The Sprout STEAM Learning Methodology
   - 6 Developmental Pillars interactive explorer (Literacy, Math, Science, Arts, Social-Emotional, Physical)
   - Developmental Milestones Tracker by Age Bracket
   - 32-Page Curriculum Prospectus download CTA with instant feedback

5. **Admissions (`/admissions`)**
   - 4-Step Admissions Pathway
   - **Interactive Tuition Calculator** (Calculates exact monthly tuition based on Age, Days/week, Extended Care, and Sibling Discounts)
   - **Multi-Step Online Admissions Application Form** (Progress Stepper, Child Info, Parent Info, Preferences, instant confirmation with reference ID `#APP-2026-8472`)
   - Admissions FAQ accordion

6. **Parent Life (`/parent-life`)**
   - Campus News & Priority Announcements Board
   - Filterable Events Calendar (Celebration, Parent Workshop, Academic, Holiday) with .ics calendar download
   - Downloadable Resources Center (Handbook, Medical Form, Organic Lunch Menu, Supplies List)
   - PTA Volunteer Committee sign-up

7. **Login (`/login`)**
   - Parent Portal vs Educator / Staff Portal role switcher
   - **1-Click Reviewer Demo Auto-Fill** ("Auto-Fill Parent" & "Auto-Fill Teacher")
   - Password reveal toggle & validation
   - Interactive Authenticated Dashboard simulation (live check-in status, daily photos stream, teacher messaging)

8. **Sign Up (`/signup`)**
   - New Family Registration Form with child program selection & terms consent
   - 1-Click Demo Auto-Fill
   - Registration success confirmation card

9. **Error 404 (`/404`)**
   - Playful branded 404 page ("Oops! This Page Wandered Off to Recess 🎈")
   - Smart keyword search routing
   - Quick navigational shortcuts

10. **Global "Schedule a Tour" Modal**
    - Available from any page or CTA button
    - Choose In-Person Guided Tour or Virtual Zoom Video Tour
    - Date & time picker, program selection, instant booking confirmation
